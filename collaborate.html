Research partnerships

For clinicians, radiology and imaging groups, and geoscience labs

You bring a question and data; I bring the modeling. Typical work is image segmentation and classification, risk and outcome prediction, and explainable results that hold up in a paper and in front of domain experts.

  • Medical imaging — CT, MRI, retinal fundus, mammography
  • SEM and FIB-SEM rock imaging
  • Small or messy datasets, including synthetic data generation
  • Co-authored journal and conference papers

02

Grant partnerships

For investigators assembling NSF, NIH, HHS, or state proposals

I join as PI, co-PI, or Co-I to lead the data science and delivery aims — methods, data management, and a working dashboard as a tangible deliverable that reviewers and program officers can see.

  • PI on a federally funded HHS / SAMHSA dashboard
  • Data and evaluation aims with clear, measurable outputs
  • Dissemination through interactive public dashboards
  • Experience on NSF- and NIH-funded initiatives
Discuss a proposal
03

Platform builds

For agencies, centers, and programs that need a data product

Secure, role-based web platforms — dashboards, registries, and decision-support tools — built in React with interactive Plotly, D3, and Mapbox visualizations, designed with the people who will use them.

  • Surveillance and planning dashboards
  • Statewide registries with review workflows
  • Geospatial maps and community indicators
  • Role-based access and controlled export

How to start

A short email is enough. These five things help me reply with something useful rather than more questions.

Email me See live platforms
  1. The problem — what decision or question this work should inform.
  2. Your data — type, rough size, and access status (IRB, data-use agreements, or public).
  3. Timeline — a paper deadline, a funding call, or a launch date.
  4. Funding — existing support, or the call you're aiming for.
